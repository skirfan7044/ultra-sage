Shaping Future Innovators.

UltraSage Learning is an IT education and skill development organisation based in Kolkata, India, committed to transforming careers through global certification programs.

Our Mission

To democratize access to world-class technology certifications and equip learners with future-ready skills that drive employability, innovation, and economic growth.

Our Vision

Create a virtuous cycle where relevant skills enable productivity, foreign direct investment, and better jobs — building a prosperous, skilled India.

OUR STORY

Beyond Traditional Training

UltraSage Learning manages a sophisticated portfolio of leading certification programs from Microsoft, Adobe, Cisco, Unity, EC-Council, and PMI. With particular expertise in academia, we craft unique go-to-market plans that drive global program performance.

Our sister companies operate in finance, taxation, IT services, ethnic sarees, LED lighting, and industry maintenance — giving us a broad understanding of real-world skill requirements.
